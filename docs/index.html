CYD Memory Combo

A memory game for the ESP32 Cheap Yellow Display, played with a PS4 DualShock 4 over Bluetooth. Flash it from this page — no Arduino IDE, no PlatformIO.

Your browser can't flash devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page over HTTPS to install.

Plug the CYD into your computer with a USB data cable, click the button, choose its serial port and confirm. Version 1.0.0 · ESP32 · 4 MB flash.

Before you start

  • Chrome or Edge on Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS
  • An original-ESP32 CYD (ESP32-2432S028 family)
  • A USB cable that carries data, not just power
  • The CH340 or CP210x USB driver if no port appears

After flashing

  1. Press RESET on the CYD.
  2. On the PS4 controller (USB unplugged), hold SHARE, then also hold PS until the light bar flashes.
  3. Release everything, pick Easy or Hard with the D-pad, press ✕.

How to play

Watch the sequence, then repeat it button by button. Each round adds one more. Finish 12 to win.

Prefer the command line?

Download the files from the latest release, then:

esptool --chip esp32 --baud 460800 write-flash 0x0 cyd-memory-game-v1.0.0-merged.bin
